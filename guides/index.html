Wedding ceremonies by community

South Asian weddings are a sequence of ceremonies, and the sequence depends on the family’s community: a Sikh Punjabi wedding runs from Roka to Doli, a Pakistani one from Mangni through Dholki, Mayun, Mehndi, Baraat and Nikah to Walima. These guides list every ceremony in order for 28 traditions (397 ceremonies in all), with when each happens and what it means.

About LaddoThis

LaddoThis is an iPhone app for planning South Asian weddings. Pick your community and every ceremony comes as a ready-made checklist, split between the bride’s side and the groom’s side, with reminders for when to start. It also includes a budget tracker with community-typical categories, vendor contacts, a gift tracker, a photo inspiration board and e-card invitations. Free to download; a one-time purchase unlocks everything and Apple Family Sharing covers up to five family members. Your data stays on your device.
